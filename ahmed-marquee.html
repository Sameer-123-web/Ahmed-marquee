About the venue

A marquee for every function on the wedding calendar

Ahmed Marquee is a marriage hall in the Harbanspura area of Lahore, hosting wedding receptions, mehndis and walimas for local families. Guests staying for the evening consistently note the warmth of the staging and décor, and the food arriving hot and fresh from the kitchen.

The team handles catering and event staging in-house, so a booking here covers the hall, the food, and the front-of-house service in one place — call ahead to walk through dates, menus and staging options with the venue directly.

What's included

Hall, kitchen and staging under one roof

Reviewers point to three things most often — the décor, the food, and quick, attentive service. Here's what that covers on the day.

1

Banquet Hall

An indoor hall dressed for the occasion — sized for baraats, walimas and mehndi nights with seating for the wider family.

2

In-House Catering

Guests describe the food as tasty and served warm, with naan noted for arriving fresh and crisp through the evening.

3

Décor & Staging

Stage and hall decoration are set up for each event — a recurring highlight in guest feedback on the overall look of the night.

4

Event Service Staff

Front-of-house staff manage seating and service through the evening; most guests describe the team as quick and friendly.

5

On-Site Parking

Parking is available on-site, though space runs limited for larger functions — arriving early or arranging valet is worth asking about.

6

Full-Day Access

Open from 10 AM to 10 PM every day of the week, giving families room to set up ahead of an evening function.
